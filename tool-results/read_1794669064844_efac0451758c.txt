   121→   121→### 3.4 Study Area (the learning loop)
   122→   122→
   123→   123→- **Chapter path** with four states: locked, available, completed, mastered. A chapter unlocks when the previous one is completed (best score of at least 1). Header search across chapters.
   124→   124→- **Chapter page:** source line (who wrote it, not official), glossary, notes with **Listen** (read-aloud: sentence by sentence, pause/resume, saved speed, Hindi voice for Devanagari text), key points, mind map (built from notes, tap to expand), flashcards (tap to flip), revision checklist, quiz with per-question explanations.
   125→   125→- **Consolidation:** two revision sessions per chapter; mock tests with review of missed questions and ‘flag’ of weak chapters (clearing a flagged chapter is rewarded); a 15-minute **complementary subject** session unlocked by a completed chapter.
   126→   126→- **Practice engine:** spaced-repetition flashcards (Leitner, 5 boxes at 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days, self-rated, cards built from attempted chapters, XP once per day); weak-topic hub; adaptive practice; mixed-topic revision (interleaving); legendary challenge; timed 60-second drills; error-analysis lab (five worked solutions); remedial lesson built from the learner’s own misses; mistake notebook with tags (concept gap, careless slip, calculation error, forgot the fact); question-paper section that holds only official links and a self-reported attempt log.
   127→   127→- **Utilities:** printable worksheet with answer key; local note upload (.txt, .md; PDFs rejected with a clear message); study reminder (browser notification while the tab is open); Study Video (paste a YouTube link, watch beside notes); Board and class guide with assessment patterns and confidence lines; CBSE Class 11 Physics outline with official facts and a link to the official PDF.

   129→   129→### 3.5 Games and labs (eligibility by band; read `vGames` for the authoritative gates)
   130→   130→
   131→   131→Number Hop, Match the Pictures, Spell the Word, Sharp Eyes (odd-one-out and patterns), Capital Challenge, History Timeline, Pendulum Lab, Fraction Puzzles, Ecosystem Builder, Projectile Lab, Algebra Challenges, Virtual Chemistry Lab (balancing equations with live atom counts), Genetics Lab (Punnett squares), Budget Planner, Circuit Lab (series, parallel, target current), Engineering Lab (beam support forces by moments), Dataset Explorer (made-up data), Build Your Own Spaceship, Coding Lab (JavaScript in a Worker with 2 s limit; Python in Pyodide with 3 s limit; tests; hints; three debugging exercises), AI Lab and AI Fairness Lab (made-up data, overfitting and bias), Web Workspace (HTML and CSS with sandboxed live preview and a checked challenge). Each game awards XP once (typically +20 or +25 for five correct in a row; +25 for a passing code exercise). Physics models are intentionally simplified and say so on screen:
   132→   132→
   133→   133→- Rocket: dv = Isp × g0 × ln(m0 / m\_dry), with m\_dry = hull + engine mass × count + payload + 0.08 × fuel; thrust-to-weight = thrust × count / (m0 × g0). Engines (thrust kN, Isp s, mass t): Chem-S 3000 / 310 / 3, Hydro-X 1800 / 420 / 3.5, Ion-Z 5 / 3000 / 1. Missions: low Earth orbit from the ground needs dv at least 9.0 km/s and thrust-to-weight above 1.2; lunar transfer from orbit needs 3.2 km/s and thrust-to-weight above 0.005. A winning design exists (for example 2 Hydro-X, 220 t fuel, 2 t hull, 1 t payload). Keep that solvability as a test.
   134→   134→- Projectile, pendulum, Ohm’s law (series and parallel), beam moments, equation balancing (smallest whole numbers), Punnett crosses (nine verified): re-implement and unit test every formula.

   136→   136→### 3.6 Core mechanics (must be preserved exactly unless Section 7 says otherwise)
   137→   137→
   138→   138→- **XP on quiz finish:** gain = max(0, score − previous best) × 10, plus 20 on the first scored attempt with score of at least 1. XP is for improvement only; repeating a perfect quiz gives nothing.
   139→   139→- **Mastery:** best score equal to the number of questions is mastered; best above 0 is completed. Progress separates completion from mastery.
   140→   140→- **Streak:** consecutive calendar days (device-local date) on which an XP-earning activity was completed; counts back from today, or from yesterday if today is not yet done. Opening the app does not count. Best streak is stored; milestones at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days.
   141→   141→- **Scheduler:** per-day session slots from `plan` (start, session length, number of sessions defaulting to the band goal, 15-minute gaps, optional 1.5x on Sundays). A chapter session is locked until its slot starts and counts once per new chapter. Busy windows are school hours and dinner (45 minutes). Reschedule moves missed sessions to the next free time that avoids busy windows and bedtime, and reports how many were dropped. Also: 7-day plan, month calendar of studied days, adaptive daily target from the last 7 days.
   142→   142→- **Weak-topic ranking** weights: 2 per missed question; 3 if quiz not passed, 1 if not perfect; 2 if flagged in a mock; 1 per missed flashcard; 2 if practice accuracy is below 60% over at least 3 attempts.
   143→   143→- **Adaptive practice:** difficulty level starts 0.5 (0.9 for legendary), rises on correct and falls on wrong; next question is the unseen one whose difficulty is closest to the level, with a penalty for repeating the same chapter; 8 questions (6 legendary). **Legendary challenge** unlocks after mastering two chapters, two lives, +40 XP on first win only.
   144→   144→- **Quests:** daily, weekly (week starts Monday) and monthly, computed from real activity, claimable once for XP, with four quest achievements.
   145→   145→- **Achievements (about 21):** quest finisher, weekly champion, monthly master, quest hunter, focus starter, steady focus, first lesson, first mastery, streak milestones 3/7/14/30/60/100, path complete, legend, flashcard habit, revision starter, mock tester, fix the gaps, any-light learner (finished quizzes in all three themes).
   146→   146→- **Focus (Lock-In wellbeing):** Pomodoro-style presets Short 15/3 (default for young and middle), Classic 25/5, Deep 45/10, Custom; 4-block cycle with a longer break; XP 5 per completed block, capped at 4 blocks per day; **Strict Mode** blocks Practice and Games, Leaderboard and Friends behind a mascot blocking screen and makes ending early a 10-second wait; auto-start when a timetable slot begins (only if the app is open, within the first 2 minutes); away detection through page visibility with a kind message; sessions survive reload; a block that ended while the app was closed for over 2 minutes is not counted; self-set daily time limit that shows a rest reminder and never locks; generated focus sounds (brown noise, rain-like noise, gentle tones) with Web Audio, no audio files; 7-day screen-time analytics. Timers use timestamps, not tick counts, so background-tab throttling cannot corrupt them.
   147→   147→- **Guardian summary:** consent flag, this-device printable weekly report; with accounts, a parent sees a summary only while the learner’s consent is on.

   223→   223→
   224→   224→### 5.4 Event-sourced local-first data and sync
   225→   225→
   226→   226→Replace the prototype’s ad hoc `mergeState` with an append-only **event log**.
   227→   227→
   228→   228→- Every learner action is an immutable event: `id` (UUIDv7), `type`, `payload`, `deviceId`, `clientTime`, `tz`. Examples: `quiz.finished`, `card.reviewed`, `focus.block.completed`, `quest.claimed`, `game.won`, `session.rescheduled`.
   229→   229→- Current state (XP, streak, mastery, due cards, achievements) is a **projection** computed by pure functions in `packages/domain`. Monotonic values such as XP and best scores are derived, never set directly.
   230→   230→- Sync is the union of event sets, idempotent by event ID, so multi-device merge is automatic and safe. Settings and schedule fields use last-writer-wins per field with timestamps.
   231→   231→- Uploaded notes sync only if the learner opts in (object storage). Everything else is stored locally first and queued for sync with exponential backoff.
   232→   232→- Provide a one-time **legacy importer**: paste or upload a prototype `lumio-s1` export and it is converted to events, preserving XP, days, results, flashcards, mocks, mistakes and settings.
   233→   233→
   234→   234→### 5.5 Ranked and unranked play
   235→   235→
   236→   236→- **Unranked (default, offline):** graded locally, counts for personal progress, never for leagues or leaderboards.
   237→   237→- **Ranked (online, account required):** the server issues an attempt (item IDs without answer keys), grades each answer as it arrives, returns the explanation, and writes the XP ledger entry itself. Offline attempts are queued as `unverified` and excluded from leagues. Keep the daily XP cap and anomaly flags (the prototype capped at 800 XP per day).
   238→   238→
   239→   239→### 5.6 Time and locale
   240→   240→
   241→   241→Store the learner’s IANA time zone. A ‘day’ is the learner’s local calendar day; weeks start on Monday; the Indian academic year runs April to March. Format dates and numbers with `en-IN` and `hi-IN`. Never compare dates as strings without normalising zone.

   366→   366→### 8.1 The adapter pattern (applies to every integration)
   367→   367→
   368→   368→In `packages/adapters`, define one typed interface per capability: `LlmGateway`, `SmsProvider`, `EmailProvider`, `PushProvider`, `OcrProvider`, `TtsProvider`, `AsrProvider`, `ObjectStore`, `YoutubeProvider`, `OpenDataProvider`, `CodeRunner`, `CaptchaProvider`, `ModerationProvider`. Each has:
   369→   369→
   370→   370→- one or more **real adapters** and a **fake adapter** (deterministic, fixture-driven, with failure injection through `FAKE_FAIL_RATE` and `FAKE_LATENCY_MS`);
   371→   371→- a Zod config schema validated at boot, which prints a **capability table** (what is on, what is off, why);
   372→   372→- exposure of capability flags at `GET /api/capabilities` so the interface shows or hides features truthfully.
   373→   373→
   374→   374→Every outbound call goes through one shared `callExternal()` wrapper that provides: a timeout (default 8 s; streaming LLM calls 60 s); retries with exponential backoff and jitter for idempotent calls only; a circuit breaker (open after repeated failures, half-open probe); a concurrency limit; a per-provider rate limiter that honours `429` and `Retry-After`; idempotency keys for sends (OTP, email, push); log redaction; metrics for latency, errors and cost; response caching with stale-while-error for read-only data; and a closed error taxonomy: `UNCONFIGURED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `RATE_LIMITED`, `OVER_BUDGET`, `INVALID_RESPONSE`, `BLOCKED_BY_POLICY`, each mapped to a calm learner-facing message.
   375→   375→
   376→   376→Health: `/healthz` (liveness), `/readyz` (database and required adapters), and an authenticated admin page listing each adapter’s status, last success, error rate and quota used.
