    69→    69→Rules for the data:
    70→    70→
    71→    71→- **Stable IDs.** IDs are permanent. If a topic's wording changes, keep its id. If a topic is removed in a later syllabus year, keep stored progress (orphaned entries are retained but hidden).
    72→    72→- **Provenance (R1).** Each subject carries a `source` line: "CBSE curriculum 2026-27, as supplied by the learner" (Physics: "Delhi Directorate of Education support material 2026-27, which reproduces the CBSE syllabus; read from a scanned book, so verify against your school's copy"). Show it on the subject screen. **Do not invent URLs.** Add a note: "Your school may follow a different circular. Check with your teacher."
    73→    73→- **Formative-only topics.** CBSE marks some topics as "assessed only formatively, not in summative assessments". Keep them in the list with a visible badge **"Formative only"** and a short explanation. Do **not** hide them, and do **not** tell the learner to skip them. The planner just weights them lower (Section 5).
    74→    74→- **NCERT chapter alignment.** Chapters follow the **NCERT textbook chapter titles and numbers** listed in Appendix A.0, shown as "Ch 7" with the NCERT Part (1 or 2) where the book has two volumes. The CBSE syllabus topics from the supplied documents sit **under** those chapters. Do not invent topics to fill a chapter: where the supplied syllabus lists no topics for an NCERT chapter, show a single row with the chapter title, `assessment: 'not_in_syllabus_doc'`, and the badge **"In NCERT book, not in the syllabus you supplied. Ask your teacher"**. Items that the CBSE document still lists but the rationalised NCERT book no longer has (Maths induction, Chemistry s and p block and gaseous state) stay as `formative_only` chapters with the note "Removed from the rationalised NCERT book; CBSE lists it as formative only".
    75→    75→- **Do not copy the Google/Vedantu/Byju's links** from any chat or notes into the app. Provenance lines name the document only (R1: no unverified links).
    76→    76→- **Advanced learners (AI 843).** Items marked advanced get an **"Advanced, not evaluated"** badge and a low planner weight.
    77→    77→- Add `scripts/syllabus-audit.mjs` (runnable, like `contrast-audit.mjs`) that checks: ids unique and stable-format, every topic has a title, unit marks sum to the subject totals in Appendix A, counts per subject match the appendix (chapter counts: Physics 14, Chemistry 9 NCERT + 2 formative-only, Maths 14 NCERT + 1 formative-only, Computer Science 11, AI 5 Part A units + 8 Part B units), and each `catalogueName` exists in `SUBJECT_CATALOGUE`.    152→   152→## 5. Today's plan
   153→   153→
   154→   154→### 5.1 The deterministic planner (must work with no AI, no account, no network)
   155→   155→
   156→   156→Pure module `src/lib/lumio/syllabus/planner.ts`:
   157→   157→
   158→   158→```ts
   159→   159→buildSyllabusPlan(input: {
   160→   160→  today: string;                       // learner-local yyyy-mm-dd (injected)
   161→   161→  dayIndex: number;
   162→   162→  slots: SessionSlot[];                // from planDayWithOverrides for today
   163→   163→  subjects: SyllabusSubject[];         // visible subjects the learner takes (subjectPool ∩ visible)
   164→   164→  compSub?: string;                    // complementary subject pairing
   165→   165→  progress: Record<TopicId, TopicProgress>;
   166→   166→  examDates: Record<subjectId, string | undefined>;   // from profile (nextExamDate etc.), may be empty
   167→   167→  dueCardsCount?: number;
   168→   168→}): PlanItem[]
   169→   169→```
   170→   170→
   171→   171→- **Subjects per slot** come from the existing `subjectForSession` (keep complementary pairing).
   172→   172→- **Topic priority score** (starting values; define them as named constants, document them in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and keep them easy to tune):
   173→   173→  - Base by state: completed-but-not-confident **40**; completed with `lacks_practice` **35**; in progress **30**; not started **20** plus a small bonus for being the next topic in syllabus order (so the learner does not skip around); completed, unrated **15**; completed and confident **0**.
   174→   174→  - Flag bonuses: `concept_unclear` +12, `lacks_practice` +10, `needs_revision` +8, `formulas` +6, `needs_help` +6, `speed` +5, `careless` +4.
   175→   175→  - Marks weight: up to **+30** scaled by the unit's share of the subject's marks.
   176→   176→  - Staleness: a completed-and-confident topic not reviewed for 21+ days gains up to **+15** (gentle spaced revision, so "confident" does not mean "forgotten").
   177→   177→  - Exam urgency: if a subject's exam is within 30 days, scale the marks weight by 1.5.
   178→   178→  - `formative_only` topics: multiply by **0.4**. `advanced` topics: multiply by **0.2**. Never exclude them.
   179→   179→  - Anything already planned or touched today is skipped.
   180→   180→- **Activity per item** is derived, not invented: not started or in progress means *Learn*; flagged `lacks_practice` means *Practise questions*; completed-but-not-confident or `needs_revision` means *Revise*; stale confident means *Quick recall*.
   181→   181→- **Fit the slots:** estimate minutes per activity (named constants, for example Learn 35, Practise 25, Revise 15, Recall 10), fill each slot's length without overflowing, allow several short items per long slot, and never produce more than the slot count allows. If there are no slots today, return an honest empty plan ("No study sessions fit today. Open the Planner to adjust.").
   182→   182→- Every item carries machine-readable **reason codes** (`NOT_CONFIDENT`, `LACKS_PRACTICE`, `HIGH_MARKS_UNIT`, `EXAM_SOON`, `STALE_REVISION`, `NEXT_IN_ORDER`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `CONCEPT_UNCLEAR`) rendered through i18n templates, so the non-AI plan is fully explainable. **Same inputs must always give the same plan** (deterministic tie-breaks by syllabus order).

   184→   184→### 5.2 The AI refinement (optional, behind the existing gateway)
   185→   185→
   186→   186→If the AI gateway exists, add a feature `syllabus-day-coach` following its conventions (prompt file `prompts/syllabus-day-coach.v1.ts`, Zod schemas, `completeJson` with **one repair retry**, budgets, `ai_generated` labelling, the "AI" marker, "AI can make mistakes" line, a Report control).    187→   187→
   188→   188→- **Input to the model (privacy):** only the deterministic plan, the candidate topics (public syllabus ids and titles), their status and flag **enums**, unit mark shares, exam-proximity buckets (not dates), today's slot lengths, and the learner's **band**. **Never** send the name, email, user id, free-text notes (unless the learner ticks "Include my notes for this plan", off by default, sanitised and delimited as data), or raw dates of birth.
   189→   189→- **Task:** reorder and re-balance the candidate items, pick the activity per item from the allowed enum, and write a **short encouraging reason (max 160 chars) per item** plus a one-sentence day summary, in the learner's language (English, Hindi, Hinglish), age-appropriate tone, never guilt-tripping.
   190→   190→- **Output schema (Zod):** `{ summary, items: [{ slotIndex, topicIds[], activity, minutes, reason }] }`.
   191→   191→- **Validation (hard rules, same module as the planner):** every `topicId` must exist in the candidate list; no duplicates; item count and total minutes must fit today's slots; subject must match the slot's subject; activity must be in the enum; reasons are length-capped and passed through the safe-Markdown renderer. **Any violation means discard the AI plan and show the deterministic plan** with a calm note ("Showing your standard plan"). The AI can never add topics, change marks or invent syllabus content.
   192→   192→- **Degradation:** no key, signed out, over budget, provider down, invalid JSON after one repair: the deterministic plan shows with a calm reason; every non-AI path unchanged. The "Refine with AI" button appears only when the capability flag says AI is available and the learner has opted in.

   264→   264→- *Ch 8 Sequences and Series:* sequence and series; arithmetic mean (A.M.); geometric progression (G.P.), general term, sum of n terms, infinite G.P. and its sum; geometric mean (G.M.); relation between A.M. and G.M. *Formative only:* special sums Σk, Σk², Σk³.

   268→   268→- *Ch 10 Conic Sections:* sections of a cone (circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola; a point, a straight line and a pair of intersecting lines as degenerate cases); standard equations and simple properties of parabola, ellipse and hyperbola; standard equation of a circle.
   269→   269→- *Ch 11 Introduction to Three Dimensional Geometry:* coordinate axes and planes in three dimensions; coordinates of a point; distance between two points. *Formative only:* section formula.
